Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Compliant with GDPR, ePrivacy & CCPA/CPRA

1. Introduction & Overview

Performedges ("Performedges", "we", "our", or "us") operates the performance mobile advertising network accessible via performedges.com. This Privacy Policy informs advertisers, publishers, and site visitors regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of Personal Information when using our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two main contexts:

  • Direct Inquiries: When applying as an advertiser or publisher, we collect contact names, corporate email addresses, company name, website URLs, and traffic estimates.
  • Advertising & Telemetry Data: In our ad-serving and attribution capacity, we process pseudonymous technical identifiers including Mobile Advertising IDs (IDFA, GAID), IP addresses (for geo-targeting), user-agent strings, and campaign postback events (installs, conversions).

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of Contract: Processing necessary to fulfill advertiser campaigns, track postbacks, and execute publisher payouts.
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing ad fraud, bots, multi-click spam, and ensuring network security.
  • Consent: Where required for non-essential web cookies and analytics tracking.

4. Mobile Measurement Partners (MMPs) & Third Parties

We integrate with verified third-party Mobile Measurement Partners (including AppsFlyer, Adjust, Singular, Kochava, and Branch) to receive verified conversion postbacks. We do not sell personal data to data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Security

Campaign log data and pseudonymous attribution events are retained for a maximum of 90 days for audit and dispute settlement purposes, after which they are permanently aggregated or purged. All data is transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.
